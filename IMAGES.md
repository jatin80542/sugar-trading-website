# Images

## What is in here now

There was no photography supplied with the brief, and stock-photo clichés
(handshakes, generic office workers, AI-generated business people) would have
undercut the whole positioning. So the site ships with **14 original SVG
assets**, generated by `scripts-gen-images.mjs`, in two visual languages:

**Macro crystal fields** — layered, depth-of-field renderings of sugar crystals,
toned per grade. Used for the hero and every product page.

| File | Used on | Tone |
|------|---------|------|
| `hero-supply.svg` | Home hero | Dark, gold facet highlights |
| `products-hero.svg` | Products landing hero | Dark, warm |
| `product-icumsa-45.svg` | ICUMSA 45 | Bright white |
| `product-icumsa-150.svg` | ICUMSA 150 | Warm off-white |
| `product-vhp.svg` | VHP Raw Sugar | Amber / light brown |
| `product-vvhp.svg` | VVHP Raw Sugar | Pale gold, lighter than VHP |
| `product-beet.svg` | Beet Sugar | Cool neutral white |

**Hairline technical drawings** — blueprint-style linework. The vernacular of an
engineering drawing suits a commodity trader better than illustration does.

| File | Used on |
|------|---------|
| `mill.svg` | Home — Who we are |
| `port.svg` | Home — Our business |
| `warehouse.svg` | Every product page — packaging section |
| `cane-field.svg` | Programme — supply chain integrity |
| `program-brazil.svg` | Programme hero |
| `compliance.svg` | Compliance hero |
| `trade-desk.svg` | Contact hero |

They are vector, 2–70 kB each, sharp at any size, and cost nothing in licensing.

## Replacing them with real photography

Every image is rendered by the `Frame` component, which is a plain `<img>` with a
fixed aspect ratio. Swapping in a photo is a one-line change.

**Step 1** — put your photo in `public/images/`, e.g. `public/images/mill.jpg`

**Step 2** — find the reference and change the extension:

```bash
grep -rn "mill.svg" app/ components/
```

```jsx
<Frame
  src="/images/mill.jpg"          // ← changed
  alt="Cane crushing hall at a mill in São Paulo state"   // ← rewrite this
  ratio="tall"
  caption="Origin — cane processing and refining"
/>
```

**Step 3** — rewrite the `alt` text to describe the actual photograph. This
matters for both accessibility and SEO, and a stale alt describing artwork that
is no longer there is worse than none.

### Aspect ratios

`ratio="tall"` is 3:4 · `"wide"` is 16:9 · `"square"` is 1:1 · `"band"` is 21:9
(and drops to 4:3 on mobile so it does not become a letterbox slit).

Crop your photos to the right ratio before uploading — the frame will cover-crop
otherwise, and you lose control of what gets cut.

### If you switch to raster photos

Consider `next/image` instead of `<img>` for automatic resizing and WebP:

```jsx
import Image from "next/image";
<Image src="/images/mill.jpg" alt="..." width={1200} height={1600} />
```

The current `<img>` approach is correct for SVG (there is nothing to resize) but
leaves optimisation on the table for JPEGs.

### What to shoot or licence

- White refined sugar, macro, bright and clean
- Light-brown raw sugar, macro, warm light
- Mill exteriors and processing halls
- Cane fields, ideally at harvest
- Port operations, gantry cranes, bulk vessels
- Warehousing, palletised and jumbo bags
- Laboratory analysis and inspection

Avoid: handshakes, staged meeting rooms, generic blue corporate stock, and
anything obviously AI-generated. A serious buyer recognises all four instantly.

## Regenerating the artwork

The generator is deterministic — same seed, same output. To change the density,
palette or composition, edit `scripts-gen-images.mjs` and run:

```bash
node scripts-gen-images.mjs
```

Expected: `generated 14`. Files are overwritten in `public/images/`.
